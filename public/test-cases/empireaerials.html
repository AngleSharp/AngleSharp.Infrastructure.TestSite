Services

We offer a wide variety of services from basic domestic TV aerial installations and digital TV aerial installations to more complex commercial IRS and SMATV installations.  Free friendly advice, Repairs, fault finding, extra points for all freeview, freesat, sky, Virgin and foreign satellite requirements. 

Areas covered

Empire Aerials Ltd is based in Cheshunt, Waltham Cross, Hertfordshire.  We carry out Aerial instalations and Satellite installations in the following areas:

  • All Hertfordshire postcodes.
  • All Essex postcodes.
  • All North London postcodes.
  • All East London postcodes.
  • All Central London postcodes.
  • A nationwide service is covered please call for details.
